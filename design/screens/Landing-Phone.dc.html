Privacy

Your journal never leaves your device.

No accounts and no database on a server. Everything you log is kept in your browser, and the browser blocks the app from contacting any other site.

No account, no sign-up, no email

No analytics, ads or trackers

No requests to other sites, enforced by the browser

Works fully offline once installed

Backups are files you keep yourself
