Privacy

Your journal never leaves your device.

LightHead has no accounts and no database on a server. The app loads once from use.lighthead.app, and from then on everything you log is kept in your browser. The browser itself blocks the app from contacting any other site, and an automated test checks it on every change.

Read how your data is kept · Check the code

No account, no sign-up, no email

No analytics, ads or trackers

No requests to other sites, enforced by the browser

Works fully offline once installed

Backups are files you keep yourself

On your phone and on your computer.

Each browser keeps its own journal. Move it with a backup file whenever you like.

Install it like an app

It opens in its own window, works without internet, and the browser keeps your data safer from clean-ups.

iPhone and iPad

Safari: Share, then Add to Home Screen.

Android, Chrome and Edge

Tap Install when LightHead offers it, or Install app in the browser menu.

Safari on Mac

File, then Add to Dock.
